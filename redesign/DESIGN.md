## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a high-performance, focused environment tailored for software engineers, algorithmic problem-solvers, and technical interview candidates. Rooted in the visual vernacular of modern developer tooling (such as Linear, Raycast, and Supabase), it balances clinical precision with an energetic, glowing momentum.

### Personality & Tone
- **Focused & Quiet:** Eliminates ambient noise and visual clutter to enable deep work, flow state, and sustained cognitive focus during complex algorithm prep.
- **Engineered Precision:** Crisp hairline borders, monospaced data accents, micro-details, and predictable spatial pacing convey technical mastery.
- **Kinetic Energy:** Subtly expressive crimson and coral energy accents signal momentum, streaks, execution, and active progress without overwhelming the workspace.

### Design Movement
A synthesis of **Developer-First Minimalism** and **Refined Dark Glassmorphism**. The aesthetic leverages deep, pitch-tinted zinc backgrounds (`#090a0f`), ultra-fine semi-transparent borders, glowing radial highlights, and semi-translucent backdrop filters to create dimensional, tactile layered workspace cards.

## Colors

The palette is engineered dark-mode native. Surfaces descend into obsidian depth while accents utilize a high-octane crimson-to-coral linear ramp.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#EF4444`) & Secondary (`#F43F5E`):** Represents dynamic intent, active tracking, run actions, and high-impact completion metrics. Used in CTAs, active states, progress indicators, and radiant glow boundaries.
- **Tertiary (`#FB7185`):** Soft coral-rose tint used for subtle badges, icon highlight fills, and hover highlights.
- **Neutral (`#090A0F`):** Deep, warm-cool tinted pitch black for primary background surfaces, minimizing eye strain across extended practice sessions.

### Application Guidelines
- **Gradients & Glows:** Primary action elements and standout headline phrases may feature a subtle gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #EF4444 0%, #F43F5E 100%)`). Atmospheric backdrops feature soft radial spotlights (`radial-gradient(circle at 50% 0%, rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.08), transparent 70%)`).
- **Functional Contrast:** Informational text never drops below `#64748B` to retain crisp legibility against dark surfaces.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy couples geometric clarity with developer-grade monospaced precision. 

### Font Selection & Roles
- **Primary & Display (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Brings contemporary warmth, tight tracking, and confident structure to headlines and descriptive copy. It carries high readability across high-density layouts.
- **Technical & Data (JetBrains Mono):** Applied deliberately to problem slugs, algorithmic complexities ($O(n \log n)$), sheet tags, badge pill indicators, and live platform telemetry.

### Typographic Discipline
- **Hero & Headers:** Utilize negative letter-spacing (`-0.02em` to `-0.03em`) to anchor the eye with robust authority.
- **Monospaced Caps:** Uppercase monospaced tokens are tracked generously (`0.08em` to `0.12em`) to produce distinctive technical badges and category dividers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column fluid grid system pinned to a maximum container width of `72rem` (1152px) for high-focus reading and interaction clarity.

### Spatial Rhythm
- **Rhythm Multipliers:** Based on a strict `4px` / `8px` baseline. Internal container padding uses `1.5rem` (24px) for desktop cards and scales down to `1rem` (16px) on mobile viewports.
- **Component Breathing Room:** Vertical sections adhere to `space-3xl` (64px) to `space-4xl` (96px) margins on desktop, providing generous void spaces that prevent cognitive fatigue during extended problem solving.

### Form Factor Adaptations
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, max-width 1152px centered, multi-column workspace (3-column feature cards, 2-column detail panels).
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns, 16px gutters, 2-column wrapping grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px page margins, single-column stacked layout. Interactive controls expand to minimum 44px touch targets.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on opaque cast shadows; it is constructed through layered luminescent planes, backdrop filtration, and translucent border boundaries.

### Layer Stack Architecture
1. **Base Surface (Z0):** Pure dark canvas (`#090A0F`) with occasional ambient radial gradients emitting faint crimson warmth (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.05)`).
2. **Card Container (Z1):** Semi-opaque background (`rgba(15, 19, 28, 0.75)`) combined with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`).
3. **Elevated Overlays & Modals (Z2):** Background (`#1A2234`) with dual edge treatment: a 1px border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`) accompanied by an ambient diffusion shadow (`0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`).
4. **Active & Hover Glows:** Hovering over interactive cards transitions the border from neutral translucent to coral-crimson (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.35)`), complemented by a subtle outer glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 24px -4px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.15)`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs roundedness index 2 (moderately rounded), projecting high polish without appearing childlike or overly buoyant.

### Geometry Rules
- **Panels & Cards:** 16px (`1rem` / `rounded-lg` or `rounded-xl`) corner radius. This balances structural grid discipline with organic softness.
- **Inputs & Standard Buttons:** 8px to 10px corner radius, matching the geometric flow of internal card elements.
- **Badge Pills & Pill Actions:** Fully rounded (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for status badges, tags, category filters, and micro-metrics.
- **Code & Snippet Blocks:** 8px corner radius with an inset 1px outline.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid crimson-to-coral gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #EF4444 0%, #F43F5E 100%)`) with white text (`#FFFFFF`), `font-weight: 600`, 10px radius, accompanied by an ambient shadow (`0 4px 14px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.3)`). On hover: 102% subtle scale and intensified bloom shadow.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Glassmorphic fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`), border 1px (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`), text (`#F8FAFC`). On hover: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` surface fill and primary crimson border edge.
- **Icon Action Buttons:** Square 36px x 36px or 40px x 40px containers with centered SVG stroke icons in `#94A3B8`, brightening to `#F8FAFC` on hover.

### Badges & Pill Chips
- **Category & Meta Pill:** Rounded-full container, 4px vertical / 12px horizontal padding. JetBrains Mono font (`badge-caps`), uppercase. Background: `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.08)`, border: `1px solid rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.25)`, text: `#FB7185`.
- **Difficulty Badges:**
  - Easy: Green tint (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.1)`), text `#34D399`.
  - Medium: Amber tint (`rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.1)`), text `#FBBF24`.
  - Hard: Red tint (`rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.1)`), text `#F87171`.

### Cards & Glassmorphic Containers
- **Default Card:** Surface fill `#0F131C` at 85% opacity, backdrop blur 12px, border 1px solid `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.07)`.
- **Hover State:** Smooth `200ms cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` transition. Subtle upward translation (-2px), border shift to `rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.3)`, and ambient red undertone glow.

### Form Inputs & Interactive Fields
- **Input Field:** Dark container (`#090A0F`), 1px border (`#1E293B`), text `#F8FAFC`, placeholder `#64748B`, 10px radius, 12px vertical / 16px horizontal padding.
- **Focused State:** 1px border highlighted with `#EF4444`, supported by a focus ring glow: `0 0 0 3px rgba(239, 68, 68, 0.2)`.

### Code & Problem List Rows
- **List Item Container:** Alternating clean row structures with hover highlight `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.02)`.
- **Status Indicator:** Left-aligned status dot or monospaced checkbox with smooth transition state on completion.
